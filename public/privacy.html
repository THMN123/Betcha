Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Betcha (“Betcha”, “we”, “us”) is an arcade app where friends challenge each other to short games. Betcha is operated by an individual in Lesotho; it is not yet a registered company. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Betcha currently uses play credits only. Credits have no cash value and no real money is collected or paid out.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the display name you choose, and a profile picture. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign up with email, your password is stored by our sign-in provider in hashed form; we never see it.
  • Gameplay data: the matches you create or join, your buy-ins, scores, rankings and winnings, and the moves you make during a match (your “run”), with timestamps.
  • Fair-play data: measurements calculated from your runs to detect cheating, such as input speed and reaction times, and the outcome of any review.
  • Credits ledger: a record of every credit movement on your account (welcome and daily credits, buy-ins, refunds and winnings).
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting and database providers automatically log technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, and request times.
  • On your device: the app stores your sign-in session and a few settings (for example an invite you opened, or a finished run that is waiting to be sent) in your browser’s local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account, matches and wallet.
  • To calculate official scores: your moves are replayed on our servers, and that result decides the match.
  • To keep games fair: detecting bots, cheating and abuse, and reviewing suspicious wins.
  • To keep an accurate, auditable record of credits.
  • To send account emails you request, such as sign-up confirmation and password resets.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

What other players can see

  • Your display name, handle and profile picture.
  • Your scores, results and winnings in matches you play, and leaderboard entries.
  • While a match is being played, your moves are shared live with the other players in that match so they can see your game. These live updates are for display only.

Other players cannot see your email address.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run Betcha. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, live match connections and server functions). Our data is stored in Supabase’s Frankfurt, Germany region.
  • Vercel (hosting the app).
  • Google, if you choose “Continue with Google” to sign in.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect players and the integrity of the games (for example, to investigate cheating or fraud).

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except where we need to keep records for a limited time, for example the credits ledger and fair-play records needed to resolve disputes or prevent fraud.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal information.

To make a request, email thaanemoletsane6@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will respond within 30 days.

Age

Betcha is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to account and credit data is restricted by database security rules, and credit movements can only happen through checked server functions. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example before introducing any real-money features. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
